How to read this sheet
The manual pricing sheet is deliberately kept out of the AACE estimate. The estimate prices the package from percentage factors at ±25 %; this sheet prices it from quantities at whatever rates you enter. It covers materials, services and equipment transport only — civil works, erection, engineering and contingency live in the estimate, so the two totals are not comparable line for line. Where the two agree, the estimate is safe; where they diverge, the line item that diverged is the one to re-quote. Overridden lines are marked manual and keep their own total through the freight, duty and handling chain.
