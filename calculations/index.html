Establish the single design feed used for every downstream calculation. The four sources are blended on a mass basis, not by simple average, because 250 m³/h of process drain dominates the 50 m³/h CDU stream.

Inputs
CDU flow50 m³/h
Oil storage flow80 m³/h
Process drain flow250 m³/h
Chemical drain flow120 m³/h
Design capacity550 m³/h
Calculation
Total inlet flow
Q = Σ Qᵢ
= 50 + 80 + 250 + 120
= 500 m³/h
Blended concentration
C = Σ (Cᵢ · Qᵢ) / Σ Qᵢ
= Oil & grease = (1500·50 + 800·80 + 500·250 + 50·120) / 500
= 540 mg/L
Oil & grease load
L = C · Q / 1000
= 540 × 550 / 1000
= 297.0 kg/h
