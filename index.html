Option 1 cannot meet its own limits

The blended inlet carries 1,560 mg/L chloride and 813 mg/L sulphate. The river discharge table allows 600 and 400. Neither is removable by biological treatment, so Option 1 needs desalination or a renegotiated consent — this has to be settled before the bid is priced.

The pond is a plot problem

At 165 m³/h of RO reject, net evaporation of 1.60 m/yr needs 108.2 ha (1,082,000 m²) of lined pond — roughly $19.48M of lining alone. Concentrating the brine first cuts that to about 21.6 ha (216,400 m²) and is the basis of the recommended option.

Hazardous area — the note that governs this package

Note 17
Wastewater treatment open basin areas containing refinery hydrocarbons are classified as Zone 0, while other equipment and modules (such as ultra treatment package) that contain hydrocarbons shall be considered Zone 2, and the selection of all associated instrument and electrical equipment shall be carried out accordingly.

Practical consequence: every instrument in the API separator, CPI and DAF oil-removal train defaults to Zone 0 (Ex ia, Category 1G), while the UF/RO module is Zone 2 IIB T3. That difference alone changes the instrument cost of the oil-removal area by roughly a factor of two.
