Open items

Clarifications to raise before the bid is priced

Eight contradictions and gaps were found in the issued documents. Each is written so it can be issued to ABG today: reference, issue, proposal and cost impact.
CL-001Minimum ambient temperatureMedium
Issue

RFQ §2 states −8 °C; BOD-0001 §6.1 states +1 °C; BOD-0002 §6 states −5 °C. Three different numbers for the same site.

2024B047-MR-0022 §2 vs BOD-0001 §6.1 vs BOD-0002 §6

Proposal

Design all outdoor equipment, instrument enclosures and freeze protection for −8 °C (the most onerous) at no cost impact; confirm MRC accepts −8 °C as the governing MDMT basis.

Cost impact

Nil if −8 °C accepted; ~0.4 % of package value if −8 °C mandated for all wetted CS.

CL-002Seismic design basisHigh
Issue

RFQ §2 specifies Zone II with Z = 0.07; BOD-0001 §6.3 specifies UBC-1997 Zone 1 with Ca 0.12, Cv 0.18 and importance factor 1.25. These are not the same demand.

2024B047-MR-0022 §2 vs BOD-0001 §6.3

Proposal

Adopt the more onerous of the two (UBC Ca = 0.12, I = 1.25) for all package structures and tank anchorage; request MRC confirmation of the single governing basis.

Cost impact

≈1.1 % of civil/structural scope for anchorage upgrade if UBC governs.

CL-003Chloride and sulphate outlet limits (Option 1)High
Issue

Blended inlet chloride is 1,560 mg/L and sulphate 813 mg/L, but the river discharge limits are 600 mg/L and 400 mg/L. Biological treatment cannot remove either.

2024B047-MR-0022 §7 vs §8

Proposal

Option 1 is only compliant with a desalination step (RO or ion exchange) or a renegotiated discharge consent. Present both with priced delta; do not offer biological-only for Option 1.

Cost impact

RO train ≈ 22 % of Option 1 capex if the limits are enforced.

CL-004H2S / sulphide inlet and no outlet limitMedium
Issue

Chemical drain carries 100 mg/L S²⁻ and CDU 60 mg/L (blended 43 mg/L) but the outlet table gives no sulphide limit (blank).

2024B047-MR-0022 §7 vs §8

Proposal

Design for ≤1 mg/L sulphide at outlet as good practice; request MRC to confirm a numeric limit so the oxidation duty is not a commercial risk.

Cost impact

Oxidant consumption ±0.3 % of opex per 10 mg/L of design sulphide.

CL-005Outlet turbidity and TDS markingHigh
Issue

Option 2 table marks turbidity, TDS, TSS, DO, BOD and 30 further parameters as 'VTA' (vendor to advise) with no numeric limit, yet §9 calls them 'maximum required property'.

2024B047-MR-0022 §9

Proposal

Offer a firm numeric envelope (turbidity ≤1 NTU, TDS ≤1,000 mg/L, TSS ≤5 mg/L, BOD ≤10 mg/L) and get MRC sign-off, otherwise the performance guarantee is unenforceable.

Cost impact

Protects the LD exposure on the performance guarantee — no capex delta.

CL-006Motor changeover voltage 132–133 kWLow
Issue

RFQ §11.2 changes from LV to MV at '133 kW and above'; BOD-0002 §17 changes at '132 kW and above'. A 132 kW motor therefore falls on both sides.

2024B047-MR-0022 §11.2 vs BOD-0002 §17

Proposal

Adopt MV (3.3 kV) at ≥132 kW per BOD-0002 and align the RFQ wording; verify MCC spare ways on that basis.

Cost impact

Neutral; affects MCC schedule and cable sizing only.

CL-007Pump standby requirement vs package scopeMedium
Issue

RFQ §11.1 requires all rotary equipment supplied with installed standby, which doubles the pump count in a 500 m³/h train and conflicts with the §17 turndown philosophy.

2024B047-MR-0022 §11.1 vs §17

Proposal

Apply installed standby to critical duty only (feed, recycle, RO high-pressure, oxidant dosing, blowers) and offer the rest with one installed spare per train plus warehouse spare rotables.

Cost impact

−6 to −8 % of pump package value if standby is rationalised.

CL-008Vendor list coverageHigh
Issue

The MRC approved vendor list has no governing vendors at all for RO/UF membranes, DAF, MBBR media, evaporator/crystalliser, decanter centrifuges and process analysers.

2024B047-ABG-DIW Vendors List R1

Proposal

Invoke the list's own clause: for non-listed items the Contractor selects per project specification, and where an item has fewer than 3 approved vendors ABG may add vendors with formal MRC approval. Submit the proposed list in this dossier for approval.

Cost impact

Enables competitive tender — avoids single-source premium of 8–15 %.

Scope note
EPCC — design, engineering, manufacture, supply, commissioning, training, performance guarantee. Delivery FCA vendor premises, export packing for 6 months desert storage. Guarantee basis: 24 months from commissioning / 36 months from delivery.
