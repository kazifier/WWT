What the 3D model shows

  • All 33 equipment items including pumps (API 610/675), mixers, heat exchangers (TEMA R), electrical panels (MV/LV, MCC, transformer, UPS), cable tray route and lightning arrestor tower.
  • Hazardous area colour rings per IEC 60079 / drawing B047-ABG-03-PR-LAY-0001 Rev 4 Note 17: Zone 0 (red), Zone 1 (amber), Zone 2 (teal), Unclassified (grey).
  • Each item tagged with its design code from the BOD documents (e.g. "ASME VIII Div.1", "API 610", "IEC 62271", "TEMA R / API 660").
  • Make/buy indicator: green = Ramsis Bahrain manufacture, violet = purchased from approved vendor.

What it deliberately omits

  • Detailed pipe routing, pipe stress analysis, pipe flexibilities (these come from the detailed piping model per PP-BOD-0001).
  • Cable route schedules, cable sizing, cable tray fill calculations (EL-BOD-0001 section 10.29).
  • Structural steel support frames, foundation design, anchor bolt layouts.
  • Buried piping, cathodic protection (EL-BOD-0001 section 10.22), fire water piping.
  • Buried drainage, sumps, below-grade Zone 1 network.
  • Civil foundations, bund walls, and the evaporation pond for Option 2R (outside plot U9).
