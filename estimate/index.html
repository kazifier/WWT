Where the opex goes
Power is 43 % of the operating cost, and the aeration blowers plus the RO high-pressure pumps are 60 % of the power. That is why the blower efficiency warranty and the energy-recovery device matter more than their purchase price — a 5 % blower efficiency shortfall costs about $109,299 a year.

Commercial recommendation
Bid Option 2R as the base case at $48.58M, with Option 1 priced only against a renegotiated discharge consent and Option 3 as a priced alternate. The equipment register carries $25,188,000 of supply across 44 tags — of which $6,016,000 is inside the RAMSIS make scope. Quoting Option 1 without flagging the chloride and sulphate non-compliance would be a guarantee exposure, not a competitive position.
