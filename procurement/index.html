Section 6

Seller recommendations

Two groups. The first is fixed: items the MRC approved vendor list marks as governing must be bought from the named vendors. The second is where engineering judgement is needed — every category the list does not cover, with a recommendation, a reason and the clause that permits it.
Rules of the list

What the vendor list actually allows

ASME U-stamp mandatory

“All Pressure Equipment Manufacturers shall be ASME U Stamp Certified.”

Every vessel, filter shell and adsorber fabricated by RAMSIS must carry a valid ASME U-stamp for the specific shop, not just the company. Certificate must be current at the time of fabrication and the Authorised Inspector must witness the hydrotest.

Governing items are locked

“The Contractor shall follow the Company-Recommended Vendors for all Governing (Critical Equipment).”

Centrifugal pumps, dosing pumps, blowers, motors, transformers, MCC, switchgear, valves, couplings and seals must be bought from the governing list. No substitution without MRC approval.

Non-governing and unlisted items are contractor-selected

“The Contractor shall follow the Project Specifications and Design Documents, and select the most suitable vendor when placing the order.”

This is the clause that legitimises every vendor recommendation in this dossier for items the list does not cover — RO/UF membranes, DAF, MBBR, evaporators, centrifuges and analysers.

Sub-3 vendor categories may be supplemented

“Critical/Governing items, where the number of approved vendors in the MRC approved vendor list is less than 03, ABG shall have the right to include additional vendors after obtaining formal approval from MRC.”

Blowers have only 2 governing vendors, dosing pumps 3. Adding Aerzen or Kaeser for blowers and ProMinent for dosing is a formal, permitted route — not a deviation.

Engineering judgement

Categories the approved vendor list does not cover

8 categories in this package have no vendor on the MRC list. Under the list's own non-listed clause the contractor selects these, and where a governing category has fewer than three approved vendors ABG may add vendors with MRC approval. Each recommendation below is written to be submitted for that approval.
N-01RO / UF membranes and membrane skidsnot listed

The MRC list covers piping, valves, vessels and electrical equipment for the process units, but has no membrane vendor at all. Options 2 and 3 cannot be priced without one.

Candidate vendors
Toray Industries · JapanROMEMBRA RO + HSU UF; strong in refinery reuse references in the Gulf
DuPont Water Solutions (FilmTec) · USALargest RO element installed base; Fortilife range is built for high-fouling wastewater
Veolia Water Technologies (SUEZ) · FranceFull train supply including ZLD integration; single-point performance guarantee
LG Chem (NanoH2O) · KoreaCompetitive RO pricing, strong regional support from Bahrain
Recommendation
Veolia Water Technologies (SUEZ) for the train, with Toray or DuPont elements

A package with a single performance guarantee is worth more than a few percent on element price: the effluent reuse guarantee sits on the RO, and splitting the guarantee between an element maker and a skid builder leaves the interface risk with ABG. Element supply should stay open to Toray and DuPont so the second fill is competitive.

Non-listed item — contractor selects per project specification (Vendors List note). Submit to MRC with the RFQ response for record.

N-02Dissolved air flotation (DAF) unitsnot listed

No DAF vendor appears anywhere on the list. The DAF is the only barrier between 120 mg/L and 15 mg/L oil, and it protects the biology.

Candidate vendors
Huber SE · GermanyHDF DAF with counter-current scraping; extensive refinery oily-water references
Sülzle Klein · GermanyCompact DAF packages, good for revamp and brownfield layouts
Veolia Water Technologies · FranceBundles with the RO/ZLD scope, single guarantee
Evoqua (Xylem) · USALarge installed base, strong service network in the Middle East
Recommendation
Huber SE, with Veolia as the bundled alternative

Huber's DAF is the reference unit for refinery oily water in this capacity band and its scraper design is proven on waxy/sludgy refinery float. If Veolia is awarded the RO, bundling the DAF into that package removes an interface and a spare-parts inventory.

Non-listed item — contractor selection. Not critical, so no MRC pre-approval needed, but record it in the PDR.

N-03Biological treatment — aeration, diffusers, MBBR medianot listed

The list has blowers (governing) but nothing for diffusers, aeration grids, biofilm media or the biological process guarantee.

Candidate vendors
Xylem (Sanitaire) · USA / UKFine-bubble diffuser systems and gold-standard aeration design support
Veolia (AnoxKaldnes) · SwedenMBBR/IFAS media and process design for high-ammonia refinery effluent
Suez / Aquatech · France / USAFull biological train with performance guarantee
Sulzer (mixers) · SwitzerlandAnoxic zone submersible mixers, Ex-rated for Zone 2
Recommendation
Xylem Sanitaire diffusers + Sulzer mixers

Diffusers are 3 % of capex and 30 % of the aeration energy bill, so the efficiency warranty matters more than the price. Sanitaire's 6 m grid is a direct fit for the 3,000 m³ basins and their oxygen transfer warranty is measurable at site.

Non-listed. Diffusers and media are non-critical consumables; the process guarantee is carried by the WWT package vendor.

N-04Evaporator / crystalliser (ZLD)not listed

Option 3 is a ZLD system and the list has no thermal vendor. This is the largest single equipment line in Option 3.

Candidate vendors
Veolia HPD · USA / FranceMVR brine concentrators and crystallisers; largest reference base in refining
GEA Process Engineering · GermanyFalling-film MVR and salt crystallisers, strong Middle East service
Aquatech International · USAFractional crystallisation and ZLD integration

Recommendation

At 165 m³/h of reject the crystalliser is a 5–6 MW machine whose availability defines the discharge licence. Both vendors have refining references, but HPD is the one whose warranty structure fits a single-point ZLD guarantee.

Non-listed, critical by value. Recommend a formal MRC pre-qualification before the Option 3 bid is priced.

N-06Process analysers and instrumentationnot listed

RFQ §11.5 requires a long list of analysers (pH, conductivity, ORP, turbidity, chlorine, iron, SDI, ammonia) and none is on the vendor list.

Candidate vendors
Endress+Hauser · SwitzerlandFull water-analysis portfolio, strong Bahrain service
Hach · USAAmmonia, chlorine and turbidity analysers with lowest reagent cost
ABB · SwitzerlandIntegrated analyser houses and systems
Siemens · GermanyProcess analytics with SIL-rated options
Recommendation
Endress+Hauser for flow/level/pH, Hach for the wet-chemistry analysers

One vendor for the whole instrument index keeps the spare-parts list short, but ammonia and chlorine need Hach's reagent-based cells to meet the 0.5 mg/L and 2 mg/L limits reliably. Splitting by measurement type rather than by area is the cheaper long-term call.

Non-listed. Contractor selection to project specification.

Pumps from the governing list; skid fabrication by RAMSIS; controls by the PLC vendor.

Lead-time critical path
Four items set the package delivery date: RO racks (38 weeks), RO high-pressure pumps (40 weeks), aeration blowers (34 weeks) and — in Option 3 — the MVR concentrator (56 weeks) and crystalliser (60 weeks). In Option 3 the thermal package alone exceeds the rest of the schedule by five months and must be awarded at the same time as the process design, not after.
Local content
Imjamal is the only Iraq-based governing valve vendor and Al-Rookal the only inside-Iraq TPI option. Using both, plus RAMSIS fabrication in Bahrain, is the most defensible local-content position available within the governing list.
