Section 4

What RAMSIS manufactures — and what it must buy

The split is not a commercial preference. It follows from three tests applied to every tag: does the item carry a proprietary performance guarantee, is it rotating equipment, and does it sit inside the approved vendor list as a governing item? Anything that fails all three is a candidate for the Bahrain workshop.
Approved vendor list positionNon-governing — manufactured process equipment

RAMSIS Engineering is already on the MRC list

The vendors list (2024B047-ABG-DIW R1) carries RAMSIS Engineering under Manufactured Items — Columns, Pressure Vessels, Exchangers, Heaters, Air Coolers with the status Non-Governing (Non-Critical). Under the list's own definition, the contractor follows project specifications and selects the most suitable vendor for non-governing items — no special approval is needed to place the order with RAMSIS.

Governing items are locked

“The Contractor shall follow the Company-Recommended Vendors for all Governing (Critical Equipment).”

Centrifugal pumps, dosing pumps, blowers, motors, transformers, MCC, switchgear, valves, couplings and seals must be bought from the governing list. No substitution without MRC approval.

Non-governing and unlisted items are contractor-selected

“The Contractor shall follow the Project Specifications and Design Documents, and select the most suitable vendor when placing the order.”

This is the clause that legitimises every vendor recommendation in this dossier for items the list does not cover — RO/UF membranes, DAF, MBBR, evaporators, centrifuges and analysers.

Sub-3 vendor categories may be supplemented

“Critical/Governing items, where the number of approved vendors in the MRC approved vendor list is less than 03, ABG shall have the right to include additional vendors after obtaining formal approval from MRC.”

Blowers have only 2 governing vendors, dosing pumps 3. Adding Aerzen or Kaeser for blowers and ProMinent for dosing is a formal, permitted route — not a deviation.

What the make scope buys the project

  • Schedule. The two equalisation tanks and the API separator basins are on the critical path at 28–30 weeks. Fabricating them 120 km from the site instead of importing from Korea or Italy removes 6–8 weeks of sea freight and customs from the programme.
  • Interface risk. The DAF basin, the scraper mounts and the air-saturation nozzle positions are all defined by the process design. Welding the basin around the bought-in scraper, in the same shop, removes the classic “holes don't line up” claim.
  • Change orders. Nozzle and manway changes on a rework basis cost a fraction of a vendor claim once the shell is in Bahrain rather than in a Korean yard.
  • Local content. The project is delivered by ABG for MRC in Iraq; Gulf-based fabrication supports the local-content weighting in the bid evaluation.

Risks that must be managed

  • U-stamp validity. The AVL note requires every pressure equipment manufacturer to be ASME U-stamp certified. Confirm the certificate is current for the specific shop and that the Authorised Inspector is engaged before the first shell is rolled — not after.
  • Zone 0 instrument integration. Fitting Ex ia instruments to a Zone 0 basin is not a welding problem but a documentation problem: the intrinsically safe loop calculations must be done by the E&I contractor, not assumed.
  • Site erection crew. The 2,200 m³ tanks cannot travel erected. A mobile crew, a crawler crane and a certified welder pool must be planned for a 10-week window in Diwanya in summer.
  • Rubber and epoxy lining. Subcontracted to a specialist applicator; the lining cure schedule is the single biggest threat to the basin delivery date.
Recommendation
Take the make scope exactly as listed: 14 tags worth $6,016,000, being the fabricated static items with no proprietary process guarantee. Buy the 30 tags worth $19,172,000 that carry a performance guarantee, a rotating element or a governing AVL status. Do not attempt the membrane skids, blowers or centrifuges in house — the guarantee exposure outweighs any fabrication margin.
